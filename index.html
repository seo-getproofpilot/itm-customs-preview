Good to know

Before you order.

Still stuck? Email info@itmcustoms.com, or book an install and let us handle the wiring.

How many lights come in one order?

Our 72-chip and 84-chip pure white rock lights sell in sets of four, so a quantity of 1 is 4 lights. For 20 lights, order a quantity of 5.

How much does a rock light install cost?

Parts start at $55 for a set of four 84-chip rock lights, $380 for a 17″ wheel light kit and $200 to $500 for a switchback kit. Install labor depends on your vehicle and how many lights you want, so we quote it before any work starts. Pick a drop-off time and we’ll send the quote with the confirmation.

Do I have to wire them myself?

It depends on the kit. The pure white rock light sets are DIY: each light has about 11″ of wire, and you wire them together. The switchback kits, RGBW kit and wheel lights are plug and play, with a module and connectors included.

How long until my lights ship?

Lights are built to order. Allow 2–3 weeks for processing and shipping.

What can the switchback kit do?

Each light has 15 ft of wire and runs off a Bluetooth module with an inline fuse to the battery. From the app you get solid white, solid amber, wigwag and strobe modes, with speed and brightness control. A crew cab long bed or rear bumper may need a wire extension.

Can I mount them without drilling?

Yes. Our magnetic mounts are 1/8″ CNC-cut aluminum, made in the USA, and fit our 72- and 84-chip lights. The T-bracket mount uses a 110 lb-rated magnet for vertical surfaces like frame rails and rockers.

What about warranty?

Email warranty claims and questions to info@itmcustoms.com.
