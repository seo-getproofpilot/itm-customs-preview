Good To Know

Before You Order.

How Many Lights Come In One Order?

Our 72-chip and 84-chip pure white rock lights sell in packs of four, so a quantity of 1 is 4 lights. For 20 lights, order a quantity of 5. The 144-chip, 219-chip, switchback and RGBW kits are sold by light count, one kit to every 4 lights, so you pick the count you want.

How Much Does A Rock Light Install Cost?

Parts start at $45 for a pack of four 72-chip rock lights, $200 for a 4-light switchback kit, $205 for a 4-light RGBW kit and $380 for a wheel light kit. Install labor depends on your vehicle and how many lights you want, so we quote it before any work starts. Book your install and we’ll send the quote with the confirmation.

Do I Have To Wire Them Myself?

It depends on the kit. The 72-chip and 84-chip pure white packs are DIY: each light has about 11″ of wire, and you wire them together. The switchback, RGBW and wheel light kits are full plug and play, with a module, remote and Bluetooth app. Or book an install and we wire it for you.

How Long Until My Lights Ship?

Allow 2–3 weeks for processing and shipping.

What Can The Switchback Kit Do?

Our 112-chip switchback kits come in 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 lights. Every kit is full plug and play with module, remote and Bluetooth app, and waterproof. From the app you get solid white, solid amber, wigwag and strobe modes, with speed and brightness control.

Can I Mount Them Without Drilling?

Yes. Our magnetic mounts are 1/8″ CNC-cut aluminum, made in the USA, and fit our 72- and 84-chip lights.

Do I Need A Deposit?

Yes. Every install holds its day with a $50 deposit that comes off your total at checkout. It’s only kept if you cancel on the day of the install.

How Long Does An Install Take?

Most installs take 4 to 8 hours. Drop it off in the morning and pick it up the same day.

What Vehicles Do You Work On?

Mostly trucks and side-by-sides, plus Jeeps and SUVs. If it’s an RV, message us before you book.

Do You Offer A Military Or First Responder Discount?

Yes. Military, veterans, law enforcement, fire and EMS get 10% off the total, lights and install. Online orders verify through GovX ID for a single-use code. For installs, pick Military Or First Responder when you book and show your ID at drop-off. How it works.

What About Warranty?

Every install we do has a lifetime warranty. Kits you install yourself are covered for the product, case by case. Email warranty questions to info@itmcustoms.com.
