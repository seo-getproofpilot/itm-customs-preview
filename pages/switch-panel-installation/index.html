What A Switch Panel Does

Every light you add needs three things: power from the battery, a fuse and a switch. Add pod lights, then a light bar, then rock lights and chase lights, and you end up with a row of mismatched switches under the dash and wire tapped in wherever it reached.

A switch panel replaces all of that. A power box mounts where it fits, with a fuse for each circuit, and one panel in the cab turns each light on and off. Add a light later and it goes on the next open circuit. Here's the Auxbeam system at the top of this page:

On The Pillars

Pod Lights

Square or round pods on the A-pillars or the bumper, for light beside and ahead of the truck. Each pair gets its own switch.

On The Roof

Light Bars

A bar on the roof or behind the grille. The biggest draw on the panel, so it gets the biggest fuse.

Behind The Cab

Chase Lights

Pods on the back edge of the cab roof that light the trail behind you or the bed.

On The Cage

Whip Lights

LED whips on the bed or a side-by-side's cage. Most have their own remote, and we still give them a fused circuit and a switch. (Photo is an illustration.)

Underneath

Rock Lights

Our kits or the ones you bought. On a panel they get their own switch instead of a button in the dash.

Up High

Cab, Mirror And Fender Lights

The lights up high and along the sides, usually one switch for the set.

Switch Panels Questions

What is a switch panel on a truck?

One panel in the cab that switches every light you've added, with a power box that gives each circuit its own fuse.

Do I need a switch panel?

If you're running more than one or two sets of lights, it's the clean way to do it. For a single set of rock lights, one dash switch is enough.

Can you add lights to a switch panel later?

Yes. A new light goes on the next open circuit, so there's no new switch to mount.

Do you install switch panels on side-by-sides?

Yes. We install them on trucks and side-by-sides.

Can you fix wiring someone else did?

Yes. Book a wiring fix and tell us what's going on. We can also move existing lights onto a new panel.

Do I have to buy the panel from you?

No. Bring the one you want, or we'll supply one.
