Plan The Day Around One Drive

From Goodyear or Avondale it's about 35 miles to the shop, and from Surprise about 45. Most installs take 4 to 8 hours, so it works as one round trip: drop the truck off in the morning and pick it up that evening, before we close at 8 PM.

Leave a little early. The drive in runs east on the I-10 through downtown Phoenix at the tail end of rush hour. When we text to confirm your day, you get your final price too, so there are no surprises when you get here.

Lit For Lake Pleasant And The White Tanks

A lot of West Valley trucks tow. Backing a boat down the ramp at Lake Pleasant after dark, or loading side-by-sides at the White Tank Mountains or Estrella Mountain Regional Park, is easier with rock lights lighting the ground around every tire.

On the I-10 home, keep it white. Pure white and solid amber can stay on while you drive in Arizona. Color and flashing modes wait until you're parked.

Want To Skip The Drive? We Ship

Every light we sell ships to the West Valley. The 219-chip kit, the RGBW kit, the switchback kit and both wheel light kits are plug and play, so you can put them on in your own driveway. Allow 2 to 3 weeks for processing and shipping.

West Valley Questions

Do you do mobile installs in the West Valley?

No. Every install is done at our shop in Mesa. If the drive doesn't work for you, every kit ships.

How far is the shop from Surprise or Buckeye?

About 45 miles from Surprise and 52 from Buckeye, mostly on the I-10 and the Loop 202.

Which West Valley cities do you serve?

Glendale, Tolleson, Peoria, Goodyear, Avondale, Litchfield Park, Sun City, El Mirage, Surprise and Buckeye. Anyone who can get the truck to Mesa is welcome.

When will my truck be ready?

The same day. Most installs take 4 to 8 hours, so a morning drop-off is ready that evening.

Do you install lights I bought somewhere else?

Yes. Choose Bringing My Own when you book and tell us what you have.
