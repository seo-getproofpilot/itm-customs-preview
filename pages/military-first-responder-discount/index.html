Why We Use GovX ID

We'd rather not have photos of your ID sitting in our email. GovX ID is a verification service built for military and first responder discounts, and it only tells us that you qualified. Your documents stay with GovX, and you can use the same GovX ID anywhere else that takes it.

Discount Questions

How do I prove I'm military or a first responder?

Online, you verify through GovX ID and get a single-use code. For an install, show your military ID, VA card, department ID or badge at drop-off, or your verified GovX ID on your phone.

Does the discount cover the install too?

Yes. 10% comes off the total, lights and install. Pick Military Or First Responder when you book, show your ID at drop-off, and it comes off at pickup.

Do veterans qualify?

Yes. Veterans and military retirees get the same discount.

Do I have to send you a copy of my ID?

No. GovX handles online verification, and at the shop we look at your ID and hand it right back.

Can I use the code more than once?

Each GovX code is single use. Verify again on your next order and you get a new one.
