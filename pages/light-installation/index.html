What Goes Into Every Install

Mounting the lights is the quick part. The work is in everything that connects them.

  1. 1Planning the layoutWhere each light goes, which way it points, and how the wire gets back to power without crossing heat or moving parts.
  2. 2MountingBolted on, on a bracket or on magnetic mounts, depending on the light and the vehicle.
  3. 3Routing the wireAlong the frame or through the pillar trim and headliner, clear of the exhaust, the suspension and the airbags, and tied down so it can't rub through.
  4. 4Power and protectionEvery light on a fused circuit and a switch, or on a switch panel when you're running several.
  5. 5SealingEvery connection heat-sealed so water and dust stay out.
  6. 6TestingEvery light on, every mode checked, with you, before you leave.

Wired Right On Every Install

Every light we put on a vehicle gets the same five things. None of them are extras, and none of them depend on who's working that day.

Fused, every circuit

Each circuit gets its own fuse, so a short pops a fuse instead of cooking the wiring or the light.

Heat-sealed connections

Every splice is sealed against Arizona heat, monsoon water and the pressure washer.

Your lights or ours

Bring a kit you bought from us or one you bought somewhere else, or we supply the lights.

Tested before you leave

We turn on every light with you at pickup, so you see it working before you drive away.

Lifetime warranty on installs

Every install we do is covered for life. Kits you install yourself are covered for the product, case by case.
