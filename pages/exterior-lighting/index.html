Every Light On The Outside Of The Truck

The black Chevy at the top of this page runs cab, mirror and fender lights with white rock lights underneath. Here's each light on its own, from our shop.

On The Roof

Cab Lights

Cab lights are the row of small lights along the front edge of the roof. They started as clearance lights that show other drivers how wide and tall a vehicle is, and on a pickup they give the truck a wider, heavier look at night.

Plenty of trucks don't come with them. Adding them means mounting the lights on the roof and running power down through the cab, which is where most DIY installs go wrong.

On The Mirrors

Mirror Lights

Mirror lights mount in or under the side mirrors and light the ground beside each door. That's the spot you step down into in a dark driveway, a job site or a trailhead.

On The Front Fenders

Fender Marker Lights

Fender marker lights sit on the front fenders and mark the corners of the truck from the side. On this Sierra HD they sit above white rock lights, so the whole side of the truck shows at night.

Beside The Windshield

A-Pillar Lights

The A-pillar is the post on each side of the windshield. A-pillar lights mount there and aim out to the side and down, lighting the ground beside the truck and the edge of a dirt road.

They're working lights, for loading, towing and hooking up at night. This Silverado ZR2 runs Baja Designs LP4 pods on SDHQ brackets, and Paul Pena's truck got pillar lights and rock lights here too.

Out Back

Chase Lights

Chase lights mount on the back edge of the cab roof, at the third brake light, and light up behind the truck: the trail, the trailer, whoever is following you out.

This white pickup got a Rad Designs housing with two amber Baja Designs S2 pods, installed at our shop.

Pod Lights, Light Bars And Whip Lights

These are the big off-road lights.

Pod LightsOn the A-pillars or the bumper.
Light BarsOn the roof or behind the grille.
Whip LightsTall LED whips on a truck bed or a side-by-side's cage.

We mount them, route the wire and put each one on a switch, so nothing comes on with your headlights unless you want it to. Bring the lights you bought, or tell us what you want when you book.

Switch Panels And 12V Wiring

Every light you add needs power, a fuse and a switch. A switch panel puts all of them in one place in the cab, each light on its own fused circuit, instead of a row of loose switches under the dash.

The Auxbeam panel we wired runs eight circuits. We install switch panel systems on their own or with the lights they run, and fix 12V wiring someone else left behind.

The Hard Part Is The Wiring

These lights sit up high, and the power is down low. Getting it there cleanly takes four things.

  1. 1Routed CleanThrough the headliner, along the pillar trim or into the mirror housing.
  2. 2Clear Of The AirbagsKept out of the airbag path along the pillars.
  3. 3Sealed RoofEvery roof penetration sealed.
  4. 4FusedEvery circuit fused.

Done badly, you get a wire visible inside the cab, a roof that leaks in monsoon season, or a light that stops working. That's the work we do carefully, to the same Wired Right standard as every install, with a lifetime warranty on it.

Which Colors Are Allowed

Cab, mirror, fender and pillar lights face forward and to the side, so in Arizona they need to be amber or white. Red or blue visible from the front isn't allowed on a highway under A.R.S. 28-947. Full summary on our rock lights page.

Exterior Lighting Questions

What are cab lights for on a truck?

They started as clearance lights that show how wide and tall a vehicle is. On a pickup they also give the truck a wider look at night.

Can you add cab lights to a truck that doesn't have them?

Yes. We mount them on the roof, run power down through the cab and seal the roof penetrations.

What are fender marker lights?

Small lights on the front fenders that mark the corners of the truck from the side.

Are white cab and mirror lights legal in Arizona?

Lights visible from the front of a vehicle must be amber or white under A.R.S. 28-947, so white and amber fit that rule. Red or blue facing forward does not.

What are chase lights?

Lights on the back edge of the cab roof, at the third brake light, that light up behind the truck.

Do you install pod lights and light bars?

Yes. Pods on the A-pillars or bumper, light bars and whip lights, each wired to its own switch.

Can you install a switch panel?

Yes. We mount the panel in the cab and give each light its own fused circuit, on its own or with the lights it runs.

Do I have to buy the lights from you?

No. Bring the lights you want, or tell us when you book and we'll supply them.
