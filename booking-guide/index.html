Good To Know

Do I ever log into the website?

No. Everything happens in the Google Calendar app you already use. The website reads your calendar every time someone opens the booking form.

What if two people pick the same time?

The first one gets it. The second is told the time was just taken and picks another, so you never get double-booked.

Can I take more than one truck at a time?

Yes. Tell us how many drop-offs you can handle at once and the site allows that many per time slot.

What does it cost?

Nothing extra. It runs on your own Google account, with no booking app subscription.

Will customers get reminders?

They get a confirmation email with an Add To My Calendar button, so their phone reminds them. You get a reminder an hour before every drop-off.
